No. 08

The Breast Series

Fiona Connaughtan

Nurse Lead of National Clinical Programme for Stroke

Ink drawing · Original artwork: ink on paper. Exhibited work: ink drawing, digitally reproduced as an archival canvas print

The Breast Series uses the repeated drawing of a mannequin to give form to experiences shared by generations of women within healthcare. Through repetition, the drawing moves beyond the individual to become a collective narrative, speaking to enduring patterns within women’s experiences of healthcare across generations, roles and time.

Originating from a single hand-drawn scribble, the layered pen marks capture the unravelling of words left unsaid, giving form to women’s voices that have too often been overlooked, disregarded or silenced. Subtle shifts in tonal value move from darker forms towards brighter possibilities, reflecting a gradual movement from silence towards voice, from invisibility towards visibility, and from objectification towards agency. While the work acknowledges enduring inequalities within healthcare, it also suggests the possibility of change.

Responding to the conference theme, Ageing with Innovation: Are We Ready?, the work combines traditional hand-drawn mark-making with artificial intelligence as interconnected creative processes. The original drawing carries the intimacy of lived experience, while AI expands its visual language through repetition and tonal variation, revealing patterns that may remain unseen in a single drawing alone.

Ultimately, the work asks whether innovation can help healthcare systems evolve—not only in how they care for ageing populations, but in how they recognise, value and amplify the voices of women as they age, whether as patients, caregivers or healthcare professionals.
