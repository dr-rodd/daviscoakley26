11.Growing into Ourselves: The Human Journey Across Generations

Keyleigh Murphy¹, Claire Doyle², Kathleen Kenny³, Margaret Farrell³, Teresa Nolan³

1. Transition Year Art Student, Presentation Secondary School Kilkenny 2. Art Teacher, Presentation Secondary School Kilkenny 3. Service Users, Carlow Kilkenny ICPOP

Painting

This small intergenerational art project aimed to foster awareness of ageing well in the community between ICPOP service users and the local secondary school. A series of conversations, workshop and quotes from ICPOP referrals inspired the painting. Key themes to emerge were: the importance of creativity, curiosity and growth, having a sense of purpose, and the value of social connections to benefit health and wellbeing. The project was facilitated by Liz Nolan, Community Connector, Carlow/Kilkenny Integrated Care Programme for Older Persons (ICPOP).

The aim of the intergenerational project: to foster awareness of ageing well in the community.

The outcome: an original artwork for installation in the new ICPOP hub: Oriel House, Kilkenny.

Our hope: to spark conversations about what it means to age well among future referrals to the ICPOP team: bringing creativity to life in a meaningful, positive and pragmatic way.

In medical terms, the project reflects ‘What Matters Most?’, ‘Mentation’ (4Ms framework), along with the MECC (Making Every Contact Count) programme to support positive ageing.
