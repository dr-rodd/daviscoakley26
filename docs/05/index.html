The Craft of Care

Siobhán O’Reilly

PhD researcher, University of Limerick

Knitted swatch · Knit using hand-spun yarn sourced from local sheep

This swatch is the final product of a process which involved cleaning, carding, spinning and staining sheep’s wool. The turmeric stain will fade over time, leaving the shadow cast by certain stitches. The piece represents the importance of time and care in communities.
