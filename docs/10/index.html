No. 10

The Hands That Carry Tomorrow

Geethu Xavier

CNS TUH

Painting

The older woman crossing the golden bridge represents every person ageing with a lifetime of memories, strength, and dignity. The bridge is golden because later life should be valued, not overlooked.

One side of the painting shows the difficulties many older people face — loneliness, isolation, barriers, and fragmented care. The other side shows a future shaped by innovation, where ageing at home, remote monitoring, and connected care can help people feel safer, supported, and less alone.

The human hand represents compassion — care, kindness, touch, listening, and presence. The robotic hand represents innovation — technology, digital health, and new ways of supporting ageing. Between them are the values that matter most: dignity, autonomy, and integrated care.

This artwork asks us to imagine a future where innovation does not stand apart from compassion, but works alongside it. To be truly ready for ageing with innovation, we must create systems that do more than advance technology — they must protect dignity, support autonomy, connect care, and ensure that no older person is left to cross the future alone.
