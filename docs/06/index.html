Professor Davis Coakley Award 2026

No. 06

Approaches

Dr. Ann Marie Walsh

Geriatric SpR

Sculpture · Base: Wooden board, prescription medication leaflets. Hands-Plaster

I hope it serves as a reminder of the importance of a patient centred and holistic approach in our day-to-day work in geriatric medicine as our profession continues to embrace innovation.

The base is a wooden board layered with prescription medication leaflets. These documents symbolise the myriad of chronic illnesses and resulting polypharmacy that we often encounter in geriatric medicine. The wooden base represents the individual who carries the weight of diagnoses, prescriptions, and clinical decisions. It serves as a reminder of the importance of reviewing medications, deprescribing, weighing up risks and benefits of intervention and striving to achieve what matters most to our patients. The medication leaflets also illustrate that we, as a profession, are innovative and embrace change as newer medicines and technologies are approved and developed.

The hands in this piece are moulds of my mother’s hands; they symbolise not only the care we provide to our patients but also remind us of the importance of our patient’s wider support network and relationships. It is important to not just see the patient with an illness in front of us but to see beyond their condition to the individual, their story, and their network of care.

In a time of rapid and exciting innovation we need to ensure we do not lose sight of what we do so well in geriatrics - patient centred holistic care.
