No. 09

Step by Step

Aoife McFeely

Geriatric Medicine SpR, SVUH

Pencil sketch with poem

This piece was made early in my maternity leave and is centred on my father-in-law, Flan, who has done so much for his family in the years I’ve known him.

This piece is a small tribute to him because, in a world increasingly shaped by technology and AI, Flan reminds us of the value of making things with our hands, passing on practical knowledge, and encouraging children (and adults) to explore and experience the world for themselves.
