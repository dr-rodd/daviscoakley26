2.You Are Heard

Lanyi Yan¹ with Grace Park²

1. Composer and singer, St James’ Hospital 2. Pianist, St James’ Hospital

Music Composition

This piece is the outcome composition of Lanyi Yan’s musician residency in St James’ Hospital in 2024. She conducted a six-week Music-and-Health project with the hospital inpatients and older adults from the community. Through this process, she gathered stories about creative arts from the older adults, and composed this piece inspired by the stories. This piece was premiered in Mercer Institute of Successful Ageing, St James’ Hospital in December 2024, and performed in St James’ Church and RDS, Dublin.
